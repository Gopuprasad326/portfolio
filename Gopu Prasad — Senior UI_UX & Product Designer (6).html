About

From web assistant to product designer.

I started in 2017 as a web design assistant building responsive pages in HTML and CSS, then moved through UI/UX roles on consumer apps, open-source supply-chain tools and enterprise software.

Today I lead UX/UI for Jethur, turning complex governance, risk, compliance and audit processes into clear, scalable experiences, using AI-assisted workflows for rapid iteration.

I also contributed to FairFood's open-source initiatives on sustainable food systems and supply-chain transparency.
